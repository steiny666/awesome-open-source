# Awesome Open Source Project List by Steiny

<blockquote align="center">A List of Awesome Open-Source Projects made by myself</blockquote>

### 📱 Android Apps

<!-- sort list:android-apps -->
- [F-Droid](https://github.com/f-droid) - Best app store for open-source applications on android.
- [Essentials](https://github.com/sameerasw/essentials) - Modify and customize your android even more.
- [Overcast](https://github.com/sameerasw/essentials) - A weather app with a clean interface and every important infomation.
- [Seal](https://github.com/JunkFood02/Seal) - Good yt-dlp client with material design.
- [Wiki Reader](https://github.com/nsh07/WikiReader) - The best wikipedia client out there.


### 🖥️ NAS & Docker

<!-- sort list:nas-docker -->
- [Jellyfin](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin) - Your own personal media service.
- [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich) - Tired of monthly paying for google photos? Just host your own photos with a easy to use GUI.
- [Home Assisant](https://github.com/home-assistant) - Make your home smart with privacy.


### 🔒 Privacy

<!-- sort list:privacy -->
- [Bitwarden](https://github.com/bitwarden) - Easy to use password manager. The best one you can get for free.
- [Proton VPN](https://github.com/ProtonVPN) - Best free VPN out there.
